About

Construction robotics for future site

This one-day seminar brings together researchers and practitioners from top universities and construction organizations to examine how robotics can move from labs toward real construction sites.

Mirosław J. Skibniewski

Professor Mirosław J. Skibniewski is with the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering and the Maryland Robotics Center at the University of Maryland. His work covers construction engineering and management, construction automation and robotics, information technology for project management, and infrastructure resilience.

Official University of Maryland profile ↗

Yong Kwon Cho

Professor Yong Kwon Cho is with the School of Civil and Environmental Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology and directs the Robotics and Intelligent Construction Automation Lab. His research advances construction robotics, field automation, robot navigation and human–robot interaction.

Official Georgia Institute of Technology profile ↗

Xiaowei Luo

Professor Xiaowei Luo is a Professor in the Department of Architecture and Civil Engineering at City University of Hong Kong. His research includes construction safety technology and management, construction robotics and human–robot collaboration, digital twins, building information modeling and intelligent jobsites.

Official university profile ↗

Hongling Guo

Professor Hongling Guo is Head and Associate Professor in the Department of Construction Management at Tsinghua University. His research spans intelligent construction, virtual construction, digital construction safety management and building information modeling.

Official university profile ↗

Jack Chin Pang Cheng

Professor Jack Chin Pang Cheng is Associate Head and Professor in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. His research covers building information modeling, digital twins, the Internet of Things, green buildings and sustainable construction.

Official university research profile ↗

Pai Zheng

Professor Pai Zheng is an Associate Professor in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. His research focuses on human–robot collaboration, smart product-service systems and industrial embodied intelligence.

Official university profile ↗

Ziqi Wang

Professor Ziqi Wang is an Assistant Professor in the Division of Integrative Systems and Design at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. His research covers embodied artificial intelligence, multi-robot manipulation, robotic assembly and computational assemblies.

Official university profile ↗

Jiadong Yu

Professor Jiadong Yu is an Assistant Professor in the Internet of Things Thrust and Artificial Intelligence Thrust, Information Hub, at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). Her research focuses on multi-agent reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems and edge intelligence.

Official academic profile ↗

Haobo Liang

Dr. Haobo Liang is Associate Director of the Hong Kong Center for Construction Robotics. He is responsible for the Center’s operations and research and development, supporting the translation of robotics, automation and artificial intelligence into construction practice.

Official university profile ↗
