About

This workshop will bring together leading scholars and practitioners to discuss how robotics and autonomous equipment can support future construction sites. With wider adoption across construction sites, the practical value of these technologies depends on how safely and effectively they work with people in changing site conditions. The workshop will therefore focus on human robot interaction, together with sensing, planning, control, worker acceptance, and the management of mixed human machine teams.

This one-day, in-person programme will invite speakers from overseas, Mainland China, and Hong Kong. It will connect fundamental research with construction-site needs through keynote and technical presentations, discussion sessions, student poster presentations during coffee breaks, and informal exchanges between students and invited speakers. Through these activities, the workshop will create opportunities for academic exchange and future collaboration among researchers, practitioners, and students.

Mirosław J. Skibniewski

Mirosław J. Skibniewski is a Professor of Civil/Construction Engineering and Project Management at the University of Maryland, College Park, USA and a Member of the U.S. National Academy of Construction. He received his M.S. and Ph.D. degrees from Carnegie-Mellon University.

As a researcher and educator, Professor Skibniewski focuses on advanced technologies for construction including robotics, 3D printing, and AI applications. An author/coauthor of over 400 research publications, he held national and international visiting academic and research roles focused on advanced construction technology transfer and management. From 2017 until 2019 he served at HKUST as a Visiting Professor of Civil Engineering and as IAS Visiting Fellow. He was a founding member of the International Association for Automation and Robotics in Construction and served as President of that organization. Between 1994 and 2024 he was the Editor-in-Chief of Automation in Construction, and he now serves as Co-Editor-in-Chief of Engineering Management. Among his recently co-authored books are Artificial Intelligence in Construction Engineering and Management and Intelligent Construction in Tunnels.

Yong Kwon Cho

Dr. Yong Cho is a professor in the School of Civil and Environmental Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology, where he also serves as director of the Robotics and Intelligent Construction Automation Lab (RICAL) and a core faculty member of the Institute for Robotics and Intelligent Machines (IRIM). He received his Ph.D. from the University of Texas at Austin in 2000.

With over 20 years of research experience, Dr. Cho’s work focuses on advancing construction robotics and field automation. He currently serves as co-editor-in-chief of the ASCE Journal of Computing in Civil Engineering and vice president of the International Association for Automation and Robotics in Construction (IAARC), an international professional organization dedicated to advancing research and innovation in automation and robotics for the construction industry. An elected Fellow of the American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE), his contributions have been recognized with the prestigious 2022 Tucker-Hasegawa Award from IAARC and the 2024 ASCE Computing in Civil Engineering Award, honoring his significant impact on construction robotics and computing.

Official Georgia Institute of Technology profile ↗

Xiaowei Luo

Xiaowei Luo is a Professor in the Department of Architecture and Civil Engineering at the City University of Hong Kong, where he also serves as the Director of the Center for Built Informatics and Smart Cities at the Shenzhen Research Institute. He earned his Ph.D. in Construction Engineering and Project Management from the University of Texas at Austin, along with an M.S. and B.S. degree from Tsinghua University, Beijing. His research focuses on Building Information Modeling (BIM), Construction Safety Management, Construction Robotics, Human-Robot Collaboration, Digital Twins, Augmented and Virtual Reality (A&VR), Building Energy Management, and Life Cycle Assessment (LCA).

He is a prolific scholar, having published numerous highly cited articles across top journals, conference proceedings, and book chapters. Dr. Luo has received multiple research grants from various organizations, including the National Science Foundation of China, the Research Grants Council (Hong Kong), the Construction Industry Council (CIC), and the Shenzhen Innovation and Technology Council. He currently serves as an Associate Editor for the ASCE-ASME Journal of Risk and Uncertainty in Engineering Systems, Part A: Civil Engineering and the Journal of Smart Construction. He also previously served as President of the ASCE Greater China Section.

Official university profile ↗

Hongling Guo

Dr. Hongling Guo is an associate professor, Head of the Department of Construction Management, and Deputy Director of Construction Safety Research Center at Tsinghua University, China. His research areas include intelligent construction, virtual construction, and digital construction safety management.

He has led four research projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, three sub-projects by the National Key Research and Development Program of China, and around twenty consultancy projects from the construction industry, published around 150 research papers and four books, obtained over 40 invention patents and software copyrights, won five provincial-level science and technology awards, and two provincial-level teaching awards, and compiled six industry standards. Meanwhile, he serves as the vice president or board member of several associations and societies such as The Chinese Research Institute of Construction Management, the Committee of Intelligent Construction at the Architectural Society of China, BIM Sector at China Graphics Society, and so on.

Pai Zheng

Ir Prof Pai Zheng is currently an Associate Professor, Wong Tit-Shing Endowed Young Scholar in Smart Robotics, and Director, National Centre of Technology Innovation for Intelligent Design and Numerical Control (NCDC) – Hong Kong Branch, in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering, at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. His research interests include human-robot collaboration, smart product-service systems, and industrial embodied intelligence.

Dr. Zheng is a recipient of the Clarivate High Cited Researchers (2025–), HKIE Young Engineer of the Year Award (2025), SME Outstanding Young Manufacturing Engineers Award (2024), NSFC Excellent Young Scientist Fund (2024), and Global Top 50 AI+X Chinese Scholars by Baidu (2022). He is a Fellow of ASME, Young Member of HKAE, Senior Member of IEEE, and Member of HKIE/SME, Associate Member of CIRP, and serves as the Chair of IEEE Hong Kong Section SMC Chapter, Co-chair of IEEE TC on DMHCA, Scientific Committee Member of SME | NAMRI, Associate Editor of Journal of Manufacturing Systems, IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering, Journal of Intelligent Manufacturing, etc.

Ziqi Wang

Ziqi Wang is an Assistant Professor at the Division of Integrative Systems and Design (ISD) at HKUST. Before joining HKUST, he was a postdoctoral researcher jointly appointed at the Creative Computation Lab and Sycamore Lab at EPFL, supervised by Prof. Stefana Parascho and Prof. Maryam Kamgarpour. From 2022 to 2024, he was a postdoctoral researcher at the Computational Robotics Lab, ETH Zurich, advised by Prof. Stelian Coros.

He completed his Ph.D. at the Geometric Computing Laboratory (GCM) at EPFL in 2021, guided by Prof. Mark Pauly. Before his Ph.D., he earned his Bachelor’s in Mathematics in 2017 from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), advised by Prof. Ligang Liu.

Jiadong Yu

Dr. Jiadong Yu is an Assistant Professor in the IoT Thrust, with a joint appointment in the AI Thrust at the Information Hub, HKUST (Guangzhou), and an Affiliated Assistant Professor at HKUST. She earned her Ph.D. in Computer Science from Queen Mary University of London in 2021, where she also served as a Teaching Fellow. She then joined HKUST (Guangzhou) as a Post-Doctoral Research Fellow until 2023, and was concurrently a Visiting Research Fellow at the University of Cambridge's Department of Engineering.

Her research has appeared in leading journals such as IEEE JSAC and IEEE TWC. She actively serves the academic community as a journal reviewer and IEEE ComSoc conference TPC member and Session Chair, receiving IEEE TNSE Excellent Reviewer awards in 2022 and 2023. Her research focuses on Multi-agent Reinforcement Learning, Multi-agent Systems, and edge intelligence.

Haobo Liang

Dr. Liang obtained his Ph.D. from the Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), specializing in wireless communication and information theory. He has contributed to the development of new engineering education curricula at HKUST and served as the instructor for courses such as Design Thinking and Quantitative Engineering Analysis. Dr. Liang leads the Operations and Research & Development efforts at the Hong Kong Center for Construction Robotics (HKCRC)—a subsidiary of HKUST in collaboration with the University of California, Berkeley.

The center is dedicated to advancing the construction industry through the integration of robotics and artificial intelligence, driving its technological transformation and upgrading.
